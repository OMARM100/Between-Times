# Project Spec

## Game
**Between Times | بين الأزمنة**

## Genre / perspective
- First-person
- Cooperative
- Exploration / puzzle / traversal / threat encounters

## Stage philosophy
A stage is a large continuous experience, not a collection of many small levels.

## First stage
A huge maze-like environment with three nested regions:
1. Outer region
2. Middle region
3. Inner region

The regions are spatially nested like rings/walls; progression is not a simple three-zone corridor.

## Region contents
Each region can contain:
- Checkpoint
- Puzzles
- Traps
- Traversal challenges
- Monsters/threats
- Exploration spaces
- Large halls/rooms
- Gateway/door toward the next region

## Maze geometry
Use a hybrid vocabulary of:
- Curved / rounded sections
- Square / straight sections
- Narrow paths
- Wide paths
- Large corridors
- Large rooms and halls

Do not constrain the maze to narrow corridors or perfect circles.

## Puzzle philosophy
Puzzle difficulty is not required to increase linearly. Individual puzzles can be easy, medium, or hard depending on their design.

A key puzzle pattern is layered information:
- discover clue/cipher/symbol elsewhere
- interpret or decode it
- return or communicate the information
- solve the target puzzle

## Current priority
Gameplay and level-design prototyping with limited 3D assets. Focus on layout, puzzles, traps, traversal, threats, and co-op information flow.
