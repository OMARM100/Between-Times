# Decisions

## 2026-09-22 — First-stage structure
- **Decision:** The first stage is one large maze-like experience with three spatially nested/concentric regions.
- **Reason:** Supports large-scale exploration and a clear macro-structure without splitting the stage into many small levels.
- **Alternatives considered:** Linear three-zone progression; three separate levels.
- **Consequences:** Level blockout must preserve nested spatial relationships and region checkpoints.

## 2026-09-22 — Maze geometry
- **Decision:** Hybrid curved/rounded and square/straight geometry; not perfect circles.
- **Reason:** Keeps the environment readable while allowing visual and gameplay variety.
- **Consequences:** Blockout should mix corridor directions, halls, rooms, and curved sections.

## 2026-09-22 — Co-op puzzle design
- **Decision:** Distributed information is a core co-op mechanic.
- **Reason:** Cooperation should require communication and exploration rather than only simultaneous combat.
- **Consequences:** Puzzle prototypes should support clues and required information being discovered in different locations.
